# How to get more Google reviews as a tradie

People look up a plumber, electrician, or builder on Google before they call. A review from the job you finished yesterday is more useful than one from last year, and it only happens if someone asks.

## Ask after the job, not a week later

When you pack up, the customer still remembers whether you turned up on time and left the place tidy. That is the moment for a text or an email. Leave it until the next quiet afternoon and most of those jobs never get a request.

You do not have to type it from the van. A set message, with your Google review link in it, can go out when the job is marked done.

## Give them one tap, or a QR code

The message needs the link to your Google review page. A customer standing next to the van can scan a QR code on the invoice or the door instead of hunting for you on Maps. It should be the same link either way.

Do not send them to a form you built so you can decide who is allowed to leave a public review.

## Ask every customer

Google’s policies do not allow review gating. You do not send the happy customers to Google and the unhappy ones somewhere else. Ask the people you did the work for.

If someone is unhappy, you still want to hear it. Talk to them about the job. Do not hide the review link from them.

## Do not pay for the review

Google’s policies do not allow incentives for reviews. A discount, a free call-out, or a gift card in exchange for a review is not on. A plain request is enough: the job is done, here is the link if you want to leave one.

## Show the reviews on your website as well

A Google review helps you in search and on Maps. It also helps when that person opens your website. If the reviews stay only on Google, the website does not show the same proof. A [Google review widget](/guides/how-to-show-google-reviews-on-your-website) puts them on the page.
